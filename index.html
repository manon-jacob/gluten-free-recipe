BANANA PANCAKES, fluffy and gluten-free 🥞

Ingredients and portions needed for 4 pancakes

  • 3 cups of gluten-free flour (corn or rice)
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 banana
  • 1 tablespoon of gluten-free baking powder
  • 1 tablespoon of olive or cooking oil

Utencils needed

  • A bowl
  • A spoon
  • A measuring cup
  • A pan
  • A stove
6 easy steps for your new favorite pancake recipe

  1. Smash your banana and mix it into a purée

  2. Add the flour and baking powder

  3. Once mixed, add the eggs

  4. Mix the ingredients together, you should have a smooth texture

  5. Once your mix is ready, oil your pan to avoid burning

  6. Heat your pan moderately , pour the dough to cook it 4 minutes per side!

    7. Enjoy your delicious & homemade pancakes!!
